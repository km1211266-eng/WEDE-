Our Story

How We Started

Kwacha Coffee Roasters began as a single home roaster and a passion for finding the best beans South Africa has to offer. Two years later, we've grown into a small roastery supplying local cafés and coffee lovers directly.

Our Mission

To roast honest, high-quality coffee while supporting the farmers and communities behind every bag.
